**Subject:** Preview planned: OV25 configurator 0.8.11 gallery update

Hi [Client name],

We are preparing OV25 configurator version `0.8.11`, which improves the product gallery and adds
two optional features.

**Live product renders (optional).** The gallery can now show your customer's chosen material and
four angles of their exact configuration, updated every time they change an option. Selecting an
angle turns the 3D view to match. This is off by default; we can enable it for your products on
request once we have confirmed it on your staging theme.

**Selected option in headings (optional).** Option headings can now show the current choice, for
example "Fabric | Zelda Oyster", configured through your text settings.

**Gallery sizing fix.** On product pages where the gallery stays in view as the customer scrolls,
the thumbnail strip is now sized to its thumbnails. This removes an inner scrollbar some stores saw,
and on others makes the 3D viewer slightly larger. [Client-specific: if your store has a custom
styling rule for the thumbnail strip height, we will remove it as part of this update so the new
sizing can take effect.]

No product data needs to change. Existing prices, cart, and checkout behaviour are unchanged.

## Rollout

Once the packages are published, we will rebuild the Shopify app with the exact `0.8.11` runtime
and test it on a staging or duplicate theme before anything reaches your live store. To preview it
yourself: in **Online Store → Themes**, open the duplicate theme we share with you and check a
configurable product page, including the gallery on desktop and mobile. Your live theme continues
to use the current approved version until you approve the update.

We will promote the tested app version to your live store only after your approval. If anything
looks wrong after going live, we can roll back promptly to the previous approved version, which
contains the `0.8.10` runtime.
